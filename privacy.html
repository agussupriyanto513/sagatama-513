Privacy Policy for Sagatama-513

Effective Date: September 6, 2026

Sagatama-513 ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Sagatama-513 mobile barter and shopping platform built on the Pi Network ecosystem. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you access or use our application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Pi Network Account Information: When you authenticate using Pi Network SDK, we collect your Pi username and public account identifier (Access Token) to establish your account session.
  • User-Provided Content: Information you provide when creating a listing (titles, descriptions, photos, prices, category tags) and proposal/negotiation messages exchanged with other users.
  • Transaction & Swap History: Details of listings, barter proposals, direct purchases, and delivery status updates recorded on our platform.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate your account and manage your user profile.
  • To display your listings, process barter proposals, and facilitate transactions between buyers and sellers.
  • To provide in-app messaging for deal negotiations and status tracking.
  • To enforce our safety guidelines, monitor reported content, and prevent spam or abuse.

3. Data Sharing & Protection

  • We do not sell your personal information to third parties.
  • Publicly visible profile information (such as your Pi username and active listings) will be displayed to other users on the platform.
  • Transaction data required for authentication is securely verified with Pi Network official endpoints (https://api.minepi.com/v2/me).

4. Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative and technical security measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration.
